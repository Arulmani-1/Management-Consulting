Transforming Financial Operations For Retail Chain

Revenue | 8 Locations | Retail Industry

How Stackly Streamlined Accounting And Reduced Costs by 32%

Retail Chain struggled with inefficient manual processes, delayed financial reporting, and difficulty tracking performance across locations. After partnering with Stackly, they achieved standardized processes, real-time reporting, and significant cost savings.

The Challenge
  • - 45-day month-end close process
  • - Decentralized accounting across locations
  • - /month in billing errors
  • - No real-time visibility into performance
  • - Lack of inventory accounting controls
Our Solution
  • - Implemented cloud-based accounting system
  • - Standardized processes across all locations
  • - Automated inventory tracking
  • - Custom dashboard for real-time metrics
  • - Staff training program
The Results
  • - 7-day month-end close (from 45 days)
  • - 92% reduction in billing errors
  • - annual cost savings
  • - Real-time inventory visibility
  • - Scalable systems for growth
Implementation Process

Our 90-day transformation plan focused on three key phases:

Phase 1: Assessment & Planning (Weeks 1-2)
  • Conducted process mapping across all locations
  • Identified in immediate cost-saving opportunities
  • Developed customized implementation roadmap
Phase 2: System Implementation (Weeks 3-8)
  • Deployed cloud accounting platform with location-specific access
  • Automated inventory tracking with barcode integration
  • Created standardized chart of accounts
  • Developed custom reporting dashboards
Phase 3: Training & Optimization (Weeks 9-12)
  • On-site training for 28 staff members
  • Implemented continuous improvement process
  • Established quarterly review cadence
